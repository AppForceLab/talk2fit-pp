Frequently asked

How do I log a meal?

Tap the microphone and say what you ate (e.g. "two eggs and a coffee"), or use the keyboard to type it. You can also add a meal manually with the + button.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Manage or cancel any time in App Store › your name › Subscriptions (iOS) or Play Store › Payments & subscriptions (Android). Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period stays active until it ends.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the paywall in the app and tap "Restore". Make sure you're signed in to the same App Store / Play account used for the original purchase.

How do I export or delete my data?

In the app go to Settings › Privacy & account. "Export data" downloads a copy of your data; "Delete account" permanently removes your account and associated data. You can also email us to make a request.

Voice input isn't working

Check that the app has microphone permission in your device Settings, and that you have a network connection (some recognition runs on our servers). If it still fails, you can always type or add meals manually.
